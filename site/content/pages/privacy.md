Last updated: 4 November 2026

Kithsong is a music library manager and player that keeps your data on your own devices. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or crash reporting, and the developer does not receive or store any of your data.

## Data stored on your device

Your music, its library database, play counts, listening history, playlists, settings and device pairings stay on your device, in your library folder and in Kithsong's app data folder. Listening history is a record of what you played and when, how long you listened and how you started it, including tracks you skipped. It stays in your library and is kept until you delete the track or the library.

## Sync between your devices

Kithsong announces your device's name on your local network so your other devices can find it. Sync runs directly between devices you have paired with a code, over an encrypted connection, with no cloud or server in between. Your library data, including your listening history, and any scrobbling sign-in are shared with those paired devices. Your listening history is stored and synced only among your own devices. If you turn scrobbling on, it sends the plays described below and nothing else of that history.

## Online lookups

When you import music, Kithsong identifies it and finds cover art by sending search details, such as artist, album and track names, to these services:

- [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org) and the [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org)
- [Apple's iTunes Search API](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), when a better cover is needed

After an update, Kithsong may also look up albums already in your library on MusicBrainz to refresh their artist credits. Your audio files are not uploaded. These services can see your IP address and the app's version.

## Scrobbling (optional)

Scrobbling is off until you sign in to Last.fm, Libre.fm or another service you choose. Kithsong then sends that service what you play: the artist, track, album, track number, duration, MusicBrainz ID and time played. You sign in through your browser, so Kithsong never sees your password. You can sign out, or turn scrobbling off for a device, in Settings. The service you choose handles this data under its own privacy policy.

## Your choices

Settings > Reset Kithsong clears the settings, pairings and scrobbling sign-in on that device. Deleting your library folder removes your music and library data. Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on your behalf.

## Microsoft Store

Microsoft handles Store downloads and updates under its own privacy statement.
