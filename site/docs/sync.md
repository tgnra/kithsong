Kithsong syncs your devices directly over your local network. There's no server and no
account: any device can sync with any other.

## Pairing

1. Open Kithsong on both devices, on the same network. They find each other
   automatically and appear under **Devices**.
2. Choose the device to pair with. One device shows a short code; enter it on the other.

Once paired, the two devices sync whenever they're both online.

On Windows, Kithsong asks to be allowed through Windows Firewall the first time, so your
other devices can reach it. On a network Windows calls public, other devices still can't
connect: set it to private in Windows Settings, under **Network & internet**.

## What gets synced

Music, playlists and edits. Changes made on different devices are merged field by field:
fix an album's title on one device and its year on another, and both changes are kept.
Playlists merge too, even when their order was changed on two devices.

You choose what each device keeps. A phone can sync everything or only some albums and
playlists, and it can ask for smaller lossy copies, such as Opus instead of FLAC. The
device sending them makes the copies and keeps them for next time.
