Kithsong is a music library manager and player for Windows, Linux and Android. It keeps
your music in one library folder, tags it against MusicBrainz, and keeps your devices in
sync over your local network, with no server and no cloud.

## What it does

- **One library folder.** Everything lives in it: your music, cover art and the library
  database. See [Your library](/docs/library/).
- **Tagging.** Imports are matched against MusicBrainz, and you review anything Kithsong
  isn't sure about.
- **Sync.** Paired devices sync with each other directly whenever they're on the same
  network. See [Syncing devices](/docs/sync/).

## Where it runs

| Platform | App |
|---|---|
| Windows | A desktop app |
| Linux | A GTK desktop app |
| Android | A phone app, and a Wear OS app for watches |

## Getting help
